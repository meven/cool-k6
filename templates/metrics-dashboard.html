Runs are grouped by their k6 test scenario. Each plot has two axes: CPU instructions (giga, retired) on the left carry the solid lines, peak memory (MB, PSS) on the right carry the dashed lines. Each of the four series has its own colour, so identity never relies on line style alone. Click any point to open that run's flamegraph.svg (with a link to the raw file).
